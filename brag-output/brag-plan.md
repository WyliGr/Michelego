# Brag Plan: Michelego

## What is this app?
Michelego turns a photo into a buildable LEGO mosaic, matching the image to real LEGO colours and producing the parts list.

## The angle
Treat the transformation like a tiny, overly-serious construction pipeline: one photo goes in, a grid of LEGO plates comes out. The fun is the absurd precision of turning a memory into hundreds of studs.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"What if your photo was... buildable?" appears over a rapidly resolving mosaic grid.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A source photo card becomes a crisp grid of coloured LEGO studs.
- The dimension counter lands on `48 x 36` and `1,728 plates`.
- The BOM rows build themselves: real colours, real elements, ready to buy.

## Outro / punchline
"Upload a memory. Build it one brick at a time." followed by Michelego.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: upload a photo. Key action: tune the mosaic size. Result: see the mosaic and export the BOM.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a warm, premium product film for an unnecessarily serious LEGO engineering tool
- Interpretation: confident motion, restrained typography, and tactile plate-like details.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f5f1ea / warm off-white
- Accent: deep near-black with vivid LEGO palette blocks
- Text: #171717
- Display font: Georgia-style editorial serif fallback
- Body font: system sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: the dark mosaic preview and dense BOM list.

## Share copy (draft)
Michelego turns a photo into a buildable LEGO mosaic, complete with real colours and a parts list.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm upbeat bed with precise UI accents
- Music: bundled Happy Beats track, no voiceover
- Music treatment: soft start, lift on the mosaic reveal, fade under the final mark
- Music cue guidance: detect at composition time; major reveal around 7s, BOM payoff around 13s
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the mosaic glow breathe with the bed
- SFX posture: sparse and tactile
- Audio-coupled moments: upload click, grid resolve, count-up, BOM rows
- Restraint rule: no noisy UI spam or meme sound effects.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The premise — 3s
Warm paper background. A single photo tile drops into a dark frame. Text: "What if your photo was buildable?" Clean, confident hold.

### Scene 2 — The transformation — 5s
The photo tile resolves into a grid of colourful circular plates. The grid expands toward the viewer while the original image remains visible at the edge.

### Scene 3 — Tune the build — 4s
A compact Michelego control panel slides in. Width settles on `48 studs`; resolution reads `48 x 36` and `1,728 plates`.

### Scene 4 — The build list — 5s
The BOM appears as real rows with colour swatches, part IDs, and quantities. Three rows arrive sequentially, then the export buttons land.

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3s
The mosaic fills the screen. Text: "Upload a memory. Build it one brick at a time." Michelego mark settles.

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, warm, polished
